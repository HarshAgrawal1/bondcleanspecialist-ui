About the company

A local crew built around rental handovers.

Bond Clean Specialist started as a Gold Coast exit-clean team. We now cover Brisbane suburbs as well, with the same checklist and the same reclean window.

Why the name

The work is specialised. A weekly house clean is not the same job as an empty rental the night before inspection.

What we stand for

Tenants should not lose bond money over grease in a rangehood or dust on a window track. Agents should receive a property that photographs cleanly and matches the outgoing condition report.

We keep quotes written, crews insured, and recleans booked without a debate when the miss is ours.

How we hire

People first, then the machines.

Identity checks

Every cleaner on a Bond Clean Specialist job is police-checked before they receive keys or codes.

Shadow shifts

New team members walk through two full bond cleans with a lead before they run a job alone.

Product training

We use plant-based products in lived-in homes and stronger degreasers only where the surface allows it.
