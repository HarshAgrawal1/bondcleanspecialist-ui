About us

Cleaning handled properly, without adding stress to a busy day.

Bond Clean Specialist is a Brisbane and Gold Coast cleaning team built around a clear quote, detailed work and communication you can follow from the first message to the finished job.

Who we are

A local team for homes, rentals and commercial spaces.

Bond Clean Specialist was built around a straightforward idea: cleaning should be handled properly, without cutting corners, and without adding stress to an already busy day.

We cover fourteen services — from bond cleans and carpets through to offices, gyms and hospitality floors — and quote each job on the property in front of us.

Our approach is methodical. We work through a property the same way each time, so the details that are easy to miss — skirting boards, window tracks, the inside of an oven — get proper attention rather than a quick pass.

We know that inviting someone into your home or rental requires trust, which is why we keep communication clear from the first enquiry through to the completed job.

Our mission

Take the clean off your list so you can get on with the move, the roster or the open.

We exist to deliver a detailed, honestly quoted clean for tenants, homeowners, landlords and businesses across Brisbane and the Gold Coast — and to come back if a miss on our side is flagged.

Our values

Detail

The job is the tracks, skirtings and wet areas — not only the surfaces you see from the doorway.

Clarity

A quote you can read, a time you can plan around, and a person to call if something needs a follow-up.

Accountability

If something we cleaned is missed and the miss is ours, we arrange a reclean.
