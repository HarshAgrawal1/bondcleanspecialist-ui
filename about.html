Get in touch

Bond Clean Specialist was built around a straightforward idea: cleaning should be handled properly, without cutting corners, and without adding stress to an already busy day. We cover fourteen services — from bond cleans and carpets through to offices, gyms and hospitality floors — and quote each job on the property in front of us.

Our approach is methodical. We work through a property the same way each time, so the details that are easy to miss — skirting boards, window tracks, the inside of an oven — get proper attention rather than a quick pass.

We know that inviting someone into your home or rental property requires trust, which is why we aim to keep communication clear from the first enquiry through to the completed job.

We work across Gold Coast and Brisbane: vacant rentals before inspection, family homes, Airbnb turnovers, and commercial spaces after trade. If you want the background or experience notes added in more detail later, this page is ready for that extra paragraph.
