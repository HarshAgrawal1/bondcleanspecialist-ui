Before we arrive

How to prepare for the visit

Bond cleans need the property empty. Spring cleans, offices and gyms need clear access to the rooms in the booking. Tell us the service first so the notes match the job.

Empty the property if you can

Remove boxes, clothes and leftover food. We need clear access to every surface, cupboard and corner that needs attention, especially for an inspection-standard bond clean.

Tell us the service you need

Pick from the fourteen services on the list — bond, carpet, pest, Airbnb, office, gym and the rest — and say if you want two booked on the same visit.

Share the property details

Property type, suburb, and preferred date help us put together a clear quote before we start.

Leave power and water on

Kitchens, bathrooms and ovens need a live property. Tell us if disconnects are already booked.

Send any agent notes

If the landlord or agent listed rooms they will check, forward that list with your enquiry so we can confirm inclusions against your property’s size and condition.

Flag damage that is not cleaning

Marks, holes and worn carpets are not the same as grime. Photos help us keep those items off the quote and the reclean conversation.
