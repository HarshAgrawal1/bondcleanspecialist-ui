Before the team arrives

Small jobs that make a bond clean faster.

These notes come from Bond Clean Specialist crews. They are sample advice for tenants packing out of a rental.

Empty the rooms first

Remove boxes, bagged clothes, and leftover food. We cannot wipe cupboard backs or vacuum edges if belongings are still stacked against the wall.

Leave power and water on

Ovens, vacuums, and mops need a live property. Tell us if the agent has already booked a disconnect.

Share the agent list

If the property manager sent a room list, forward it with the quote. We match their wording so the inspection is simpler.

Flag damage early

Broken tiles, stained carpets, and wall holes are not a cleaning issue. Photos help us keep those items off the reclean promise.

Book before the final walk-through

A clean two days before inspection leaves time for a reclean if the agent notes a missed track or shelf.

Pets and parking

Tell us about pets still on site and where a van can stand for an hour. Tight basement parks change how we load machines.
