Our professional cleaning services

Residential and commercial cleaning, tailored to the property.

At Bond Clean Specialist, we provide professional residential and commercial cleaning services for tenants, homeowners, property managers, real estate agencies, builders and businesses. Our focus is on reliable service, attention to detail and high-quality cleaning tailored to each property’s requirements.

01 · Bond cleaning

End of lease cleaning

Moving out of a rental property? Our professional Bond Cleaning / End of Lease Cleaning service provides a comprehensive clean to help prepare your property for the final inspection and handover.

Our team focuses on commonly inspected areas throughout the property, including kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas.

Cleaning can include: kitchens and bathrooms, cooktop and rangehood, cupboards and drawers, bedrooms and living areas, skirting boards, doors and frames, floors, accessible internal windows, tracks and sills, and general dust and grime removal.

02 · Carpet steam cleaning

Refresh your carpets

Refresh your carpets with our professional Carpet Steam Cleaning service. Using professional equipment, we provide deep extraction cleaning designed to remove accumulated dirt, dust and grime and improve the overall freshness and appearance of your carpets.

Perfect for homes, rental properties, offices, Airbnb accommodation and end-of-lease cleaning.

Stain-removal results depend on the carpet material, age, condition and type of staining.

03 · Spring cleaning

Flexible hourly-rate cleaning

Give your home a fresh start with our professional Spring Cleaning service.

Unlike our package-based services, Spring Cleaning is charged at an hourly rate, giving customers the flexibility to choose how much cleaning they require and which areas they would like our team to prioritise.

Whether you need a few specific areas cleaned or a more detailed whole-home refresh, our cleaners can work through your priorities during the booked time.

Cleaning can include: kitchen cleaning, bathroom cleaning, dusting and wiping surfaces, bedrooms and living areas, vacuuming and mopping, skirting boards, doors and frames, accessible windows and tracks, and other agreed general cleaning tasks.

Pricing is charged at an hourly rate. Final cost depends on the number of cleaners, time required, property condition and requested cleaning tasks. Specialised services can be quoted separately.

As this is an hourly service, completion of all requested tasks depends on the time booked and the condition of the property. Customers are encouraged to provide their priority areas when booking.

04 · Pest control & flea treatment

Treatment tailored to the property

Our Pest Control & Flea Treatment service is suitable for residential properties, rental properties and end-of-lease requirements where pest or flea treatment is required.

Treatment can be tailored to the property’s requirements and the type of pest concern.

Suitable for: homes, rental properties, investment properties, pet properties and end-of-lease requirements.

05 · Oven & BBQ cleaning

Grease broken down, finish restored

Grease, burnt food and carbon build-up can make ovens and BBQs difficult to clean. Our professional Oven & BBQ Cleaning service provides detailed cleaning to help restore a cleaner and more presentable finish.

Depending on the appliance, cleaning can include internal surfaces, racks, trays, doors, glass and accessible external surfaces.

06 · Airbnb cleaning

Guest-ready between stays

Give every guest a great first impression with professional Airbnb Cleaning.

We help hosts and property managers prepare their accommodation between guest stays, focusing on cleanliness, presentation and consistency.

Cleaning can include bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, living areas, floors, surfaces and other agreed turnover requirements. One-off or recurring cleaning arrangements can be organised.

07 · Deep cleaning

When regular cleaning isn’t enough

When regular cleaning isn’t enough, our Deep Cleaning service provides additional attention to areas affected by accumulated dirt, dust, grease and grime.

The service can be customised according to your property’s size, condition and cleaning requirements.

Ideal for houses, apartments, property refreshes, pre-event cleaning and homes that haven’t received professional cleaning for some time.

08 · Builder cleaning

Post-construction cleaning

Our professional Builder Cleaning service helps transform newly built and renovated properties into clean, presentation-ready spaces.

We can assist with construction dust and general building residue on suitable surfaces and prepare the property for inspection, handover, sale or occupancy.

Suitable for builders, developers, contractors, homeowners and property managers.

09 · Upholstery cleaning

Sofas, chairs and suitable fabric furniture

Refresh your sofas, chairs and suitable fabric furniture with professional Upholstery Cleaning.

Our cleaning process can help remove accumulated dirt and dust and improve the overall appearance and freshness of suitable upholstery.

The material and condition should be assessed before cleaning to determine the appropriate cleaning method.

Restore the appearance of your outdoor areas with professional Pressure Washing.

Our pressure washing service helps remove accumulated dirt, grime and organic build-up from suitable exterior surfaces.

Ideal for driveways, pathways, concrete areas, patios and selected exterior surfaces.
