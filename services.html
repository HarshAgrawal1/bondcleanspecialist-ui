Bond cleaning

End of lease cleaning done right

A bond clean needs to hold up to a landlord or agent’s inspection, which means covering areas a standard clean often misses. We work through your property methodically, from skirting boards to splashbacks.

Detailed inspection areas

A clean covering the areas typically checked at inspection, not just the rooms you walk through first.

One less task while you move

Packing, removalists and address changes are enough. We take the full property clean off that list.

Consistent from start to finish

The same thorough approach through the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas and floors.

Why end-of-lease cleaning is stressful

Moving out comes with a long list of tasks, and a full property clean is usually the most time-consuming one. Between organising your move and settling into a new place, finding hours to scrub an entire property is difficult — especially when it needs to meet an inspection standard.

What to prepare before we arrive

It helps to have the property empty of your belongings before your bond clean, giving us clear access to every surface, cupboard, and corner that needs attention.

How professional cleaning helps

Rather than trying to fit a full clean around packing and moving logistics, Bond Clean Specialist takes on the job directly. We work through the property with a consistent process, so nothing gets missed in the rush of moving day.

Areas typically covered

  • Kitchen: benchtops, cupboards (inside and out), splashbacks, and appliance exteriors
  • Bathrooms: tiles, grout, showers, vanities, and mirrors
  • Bedrooms and living areas: skirting boards, light switches, and window tracks
  • Floors: vacuumed and mopped throughout
  • Surfaces: dusted and wiped, including built-up grime in less obvious spots
  • Oven: cleaned as part of your bond clean or booked separately

Exact inclusions can be confirmed with our team based on your property’s size and condition.

Spring cleaning

A genuine reset for your home

A regular clean keeps a home looking tidy on the surface, but it isn’t designed to reach everything. Skirting boards, window tracks, the inside of the oven, behind furniture — these areas build up gradually and rarely get the attention they need in a normal week.

A proper spring clean is different. It’s a chance to go through the whole property and deal with what’s been overlooked, leaving every room feeling noticeably fresher rather than just recently tidied. It’s a good option ahead of a change of season, before hosting guests, or simply when a home feels like it needs more than a surface-level clean.

  • Reaches the spots regular cleaning tends to skip
  • Leaves your whole home feeling properly fresh
  • A good option before guests, seasons change, or just when it’s time

Everyday tidy versus a reset

Everyday cleaning keeps things tidy. A spring clean gets behind appliances, inside cupboards and along skirting boards.

On its own or with a bond clean

Oven cleaning can be booked as a standalone visit or added to your end of lease clean.

Oven cleaning

Grease and grime, properly removed

Grease and food residue build up inside an oven every time it’s used, and over weeks or months it hardens into a layer that’s genuinely difficult to remove. Standard household cleaning products often aren’t strong enough, and scrubbing at baked-on grime can take far longer than it should.

Professional oven cleaning deals with this properly — breaking down built-up residue on racks, trays, and interior surfaces without resorting to harsh scrubbing that can damage the finish. The result is an oven that looks properly cleaned and is easier to keep that way going forward.

  • Removes built-up grease without damaging surfaces
  • Saves you the scrubbing and the harsh fumes
  • Can be booked on its own or added to a bond clean
