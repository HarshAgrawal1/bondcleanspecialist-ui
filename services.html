Our professional cleaning services

Residential and commercial cleaning, tailored to the property.

At Bond Clean Specialist, we provide professional residential and commercial cleaning services for tenants, homeowners, property managers, real estate agencies, builders and businesses. Our focus is on reliable service, attention to detail and high-quality cleaning tailored to each property’s requirements.

01 · Bond cleaning

End of lease cleaning

Moving out of a rental? Our bond cleaning is a detailed end-of-lease clean designed around the areas agents commonly check — kitchens, bathrooms, floors, tracks and the oven — so you can hand the keys back without spending your last days scrubbing.

We work through vacant houses, units and townhouses across Brisbane and the Gold Coast. The quote is based on the property in front of us, not a one-size package that leaves extras to guesswork.

02 · Carpet steam cleaning

Fresh, clean carpets

Professional extraction cleaning for homes, rentals, offices and Airbnb stays. We use professional equipment to pull out accumulated dirt and leave carpets looking and smelling fresher.

Tell us the rooms, any stains and whether the carpets sit with a bond clean or as their own visit so the quote matches the work.

03 · Spring cleaning

Flexible hourly-rate cleaning

Spring cleaning is billed at an hourly rate so you choose how much time you need and which rooms to prioritise — a reset when a regular tidy is no longer enough.

Unlike package services, you set the hours and the order of rooms. Our team works through your list during the booked time.

04 · Oven & BBQ cleaning

Remove grease. Restore shine.

Detailed cleaning for ovens and barbecues — racks, trays, glass and hotplates — either with a bond clean or as its own visit.

Grease and carbon build-up are the jobs most people put off. We take the appliance apart as far as a standard clean allows and restore a presentable finish.

05 · Airbnb cleaning

Guest ready, every time

Turnover cleaning for hosts and property managers who need the stay to look ready — not recently vacated — before the next check-in.

We work through bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, living areas, floors and surfaces to a consistent standard you can repeat.

06 · Deep cleaning

When regular cleaning is not enough

A heavier clean when dirt, grease and neglect have built up — houses, apartments, pre-event resets and homes that have not had a professional visit in a long time.

The work is customised to size, condition and the rooms you want pushed harder than a spring clean hour count.

07 · Builder cleaning

Post-construction cleaning

Post-construction and renovation cleaning to get dust off surfaces and present the property for inspection, handover, sale or move-in.

We can assist with construction dust and general building residue on suitable surfaces.

08 · Upholstery cleaning

Refresh your furniture

Extraction cleaning to lift dirt from suitable fabric furniture and leave it looking and smelling fresher.

The material and condition should be assessed before cleaning so the method matches the fabric.

09 · Pressure washing

Outdoor surfaces, restored

Restore concrete, paths, patios and selected exterior surfaces by removing dirt, grime and organic build-up.

Ideal for driveways, pathways, concrete areas and patios that have gone grey or slippery.

10 · Gym cleaning

A cleaner workout space

Commercial cleaning for gyms that need a presentable floor, wiped equipment and bathrooms that keep up with member traffic.

Cleaning can include equipment exteriors, floors, mirrors, reception, bathrooms, change rooms, common areas and high-touch surfaces.

11 · Office cleaning

One-off or recurring

One-off or recurring cleaning for offices — workstations, meeting rooms, kitchens, bathrooms and common areas.

We provide flexible cleaning for workplaces that need a clear after-hours tidy or a one-off reset.

12 · Pub & hospitality cleaning

After-trade presentation

Cleaning for pubs, restaurants, cafés and bars that need dining rooms, floors, bathrooms and bar areas ready for the next service.

Schedules can be arranged around your operating hours so the venue is presented before open.

13 · Retail store cleaning

Ready before open

Cleaning for shops, showrooms and customer-facing spaces that need floors, counters and fitting rooms looking ready.

Work can include floors, counters, fitting rooms, mirrors, entrances, staff areas, bathrooms and high-touch surfaces.

14 · Pest control & flea treatment

Homes and rentals

Treatment for homes, rentals and end-of-lease jobs where pests or fleas need handling before the next occupant moves in.

We inspect first, then treat the rooms that need it. Preparation notes and any follow-up sit with the quote so you know what to expect.
