Bond Clean Specialist

Cleaning services for rentals and family homes.

Book one service or stack extras on the same day. Every job is quoted before we arrive.

01

Bond cleaning

End-of-lease work for units, houses, and townhouses. We clean kitchens, bathrooms, living rooms, and bedrooms to a landlord-style list so the handover photos look complete.

Add carpet steam or pest treatment if the agent asked for them in writing.

Best for move-out dates

Book 3 to 5 days before the inspection so a reclean still fits.

Hourly deep clean

Choose 2 to 8 hours. We start with kitchens and wet areas.

02

Spring cleaning

A reset for homes that are lived in. Cupboards, skirting, windows you can reach, and floors get a full pass without the empty-property requirement of a bond clean.

03

Oven cleaning

Trays, racks, glass, and the cavity are degreased. We can include the rangehood filters if they lift out safely.

Often added to a bond clean when the agent mentions baked-on grease.

Good to know

  • Self-clean ovens still need a manual finish
  • Broken glass doors are not serviced
  • Allow extra time for double ovens
04

Carpet cleaning

Hot-water extraction for bedrooms, halls, and living rooms. We ask pets and furniture details before we quote so dry time is realistic.

05

BBQ cleaning

Hotplates, hoods, and drip trays on balcony and backyard units. Heavy rust or broken ignitions sit outside the standard add-on.
