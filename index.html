Questions

Straight answers before you book.

What is bond cleaning?

It is a deep exit clean of a rental so the property meets the condition expected at handover. Tenants use it to support a full bond refund.

Why use Bond Clean Specialist?

You get a written checklist, insured local cleaners, and a timed reclean if the agent finds a gap in our work.

Do you offer a bond-back promise?

Yes. If our clean is the issue, we return within 72 hours at no charge. Terms apply and we explain them on the quote.

How is the price set?

Size, condition, and extras such as carpet or pest work change the figure. Send the form and we reply with a fixed range.

How long does a clean take?

Most units take a few hours. Larger houses can run most of the day. We confirm the window when we book.

Should the property be empty?

Yes if you can. Remove boxes and heavy furniture so we can reach walls, carpets, and cupboard interiors.
