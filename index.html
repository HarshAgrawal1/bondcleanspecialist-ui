Questions

Straight answers before you book.

What is bond cleaning?

Bond cleaning, also known as end of lease cleaning, is a detailed clean of a rental property carried out when a tenant moves out. It’s designed to cover the areas typically checked during a property inspection.

What does bond cleaning include?

A bond clean generally covers the kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, and floors, including detailed work like skirting boards, window tracks, and oven cleaning. Exact inclusions can vary depending on the property.

Do you provide spring cleaning?

Yes. Our spring cleaning service is designed to go beyond a regular clean, reaching the areas that build up over time, such as behind appliances and inside cupboards.

Can you clean ovens?

Yes. Oven cleaning is available as part of a bond clean or as a standalone service, and covers built-up grease and residue inside and out.

How can I request a quote?

Get in touch with a few details about your property and the service you need, and we’ll provide a quote based on your specific requirements. Use the form on this page or call 1300 555 014.

How should I prepare for a bond clean?

It helps to have the property empty of your belongings before we arrive, giving us clear access to every area that needs to be cleaned.

What areas do you service?

Bond Clean Specialist works across the Gold Coast and nearby Brisbane suburbs. If your suburb is not listed, call us and we will tell you whether we can book it.

Do you offer a bond-back guarantee?

If something we cleaned is flagged after the job, contact us with the inspection notes. Where the miss is ours, we will arrange a reclean. Exact terms are confirmed with your quote.
